Analysis

What the verification chain establishes

The same flight code is checked four ways. CBMC proves SGO v2 (all properties, both tree models), the EKF step (574 checks) and the generated tree (47 checks) free of runtime errors for any float input, and the tree proof discharges exactly the output contract the SGO proof assumes, so tree + SGO satisfies the SGO invariants. On the host the C EKF and the C tree match their Python references (0 and 0 mismatches), and on the STM32F411 the full 1 Hz chain matches the host model (0 mismatches) in 4687 cycles, 293 µs at 16 MHz, under 0.1 % of a 1 s control period.

Learning and distillation

All 10 defender seeds converged. Distilling the defender into a depth-12 tree only works when the tree is trained on the states its own closed loop visits: behaviour cloning on uniform samples reaches 41.5 % closed-loop fidelity, severity-weighted DAgger 94.8 ± 0.71 % over 10 seeds, which meets the plan's 1.5 pp CI gate. Uniform fidelity of the same trees is 78.9 %, below the closed-loop figure, so it does not describe how the deployed tree behaves. The adversarial stress tester finds about 4.8× more failing scenarios than random fuzzing at the same simulation budget, which supports keeping it as a test tool.

Safety outcomes and gaps

Mean coverage over the 17 modes is 79 % (xjtu), 79 % (o2_sso_1030), 87 % (o3_dawn_dusk). The lowest are BCR_short circuit (3 %), BAT_degradation (49 %), SA_partial component or branch short circuit (51 %), SA_partial component or branch open circuit (52 %). Two causes stand out. First, observability: an array fault is invisible in eclipse and battery degradation is subtle, so a memoryless policy on six instantaneous inputs responds late or not at all. Second, a missing invariant: for the severity-1 BCR short the SGO has no overvoltage rule, and overcharge occurs in 46 % of runs (no FDIR 70 %, expert 12 %). Every mode pays a false-action rate of about 6 % of fault-free steps.

ADR-0018 proposes an SGO v3 overvoltage invariant. In simulation, a pack-average threshold of 4.20 V lowers BCR-short overcharge from 46 % to 8 % with an unchanged fault-free hazard share (0.3 %). The hazard threshold itself (4.25 V) does nothing on the average, because one cell of the 8S string crosses it first: per-cell taps are the robust fix.

Transfer to XJTU-SPS

On a power system it never saw, the deployed tree + SGO reaches event-wise F0.5 = 0.60 (recall 1.00, precision 0.55) with measured temperature and 0.64 with temperature fixed, and raises alarms on 48.8 % of the normal segment. With alarms on for that much of the normal data, the high recall mostly reflects how often the alarm is on: the tree does not transfer as a detector to this 4S system without re-scaling its inputs. Treat this as a domain-shift probe, not a benchmark score: the per-unit bases and capacity are assumed (ISS-030) and the temperature confound (ISS-029) shows in the gap between the two variants.

Processor-in-the-loop

Across 115 closed-loop runs with the STM32F411 acting on the plant, the lowest mean action agreement between the board and the software model in any case is 98.6 %. Under corrupted telemetry and memory upsets (C3) the SGO flagged every injected case (C3a: 0x10, C3b: 0x10, C3c: 0x10, C3d: 0x10, C3e: 0x11) and the EKF guards acted 465 times; the board kept answering every frame.

Threats to validity

The plant is an equivalent-circuit stand-in fitted to a DFN model of a commercial cell (Chen2020), not the flight cell; orbit eclipse times, EPS sizing and fault magnitudes are assumed until M2; FMECA severities and occurrence rates are proposals, so residual risk is a relative figure; coverage is defined by a 10 min response window and favours policies that act often; in leave-one-seed-out the EKF band covers 12 of 13 profiles and the rest stay under-covered (ISS-025); all MCU timings are at 16 MHz with zero flash wait states.

Next steps

Decide ADR-0018 (overvoltage invariant, cell taps). Supply FMECA rates and traced thresholds (M2). Give the tree short input history, which targets the observability gaps. Build the DAQ board and the 8S1P pack, then repeat the PIL cases as true hardware-in-the-loop.

4.1 · SoC estimator

Pulse-fit voltage error fell from 9.08 mV (one parameter set, C/20 OCV, the 17 Oct baseline) to 2.61 mV with SoC-scheduled tables and an OCV corrected to relaxed rest voltages, which sit 8.0–12.7 mV above the C/20 curve. With the EKF noise calibrated on 9 pooled DFN profiles, every variant covers the held-out profiles; the selected one (scheduled restocv) gives the sharpest band.

4.4 · Simulation environment

16384 spacecraft on the GPU (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080 Laptop GPU), 8S packs of SoC-scheduled 2RC cells with spread and ageing, the onboard EKF in the loop, 17 FMECA fault classes. Settled EKF error p95 0.31 % SoC on fault-free packs; 4.34e+05 spacecraft-steps per second eager, about 6× more with CUDA graphs.

4.5 · Defender

10 seeds of the multi-head defender (6 inputs → S, B, L), severity-weighted cross-entropy; all converged. Validation accuracy (mean ± 95 % CI): S 94.0 ± 0.02 %, B 96.9 ± 0.02 %, L 79.9 ± 0.30 %. The load head is hardest: several faults look the same in six instantaneous inputs.
